About Me

I’m a developer with a strong foundation in computer science and a passion for building intelligent, efficient systems. Backed by multiple degrees, I developed my foundation in computer science fundamentals and artificial intelligence during my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science. During my studies, I built several AI-driven projects that sparked my ongoing interest in automation, machine learning, and intelligent systems design.

Since graduating, I’ve applied that technical foundation in real-world environments—developing custom GPTs, designing AI-powered automation tools, and creating systems that streamline day-to-day processes such as scheduling, data entry, and reporting. My work sits at the intersection of technology and problem-solving: using AI not as a buzzword, but as a means to make workflows faster, smarter, and more intuitive.

Whether it’s automating complex scheduling pipelines or building adaptive AI agents tailored to specific business needs, my goal is to turn advanced technologies into accessible, practical tools that create measurable value.
